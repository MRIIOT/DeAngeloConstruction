What we are about

Quality craftsmanship, affordable solutions.

DeAngelo Construction is a family-owned business proudly serving Barrington, IL and the surrounding Chicagoland area. With a commitment to quality craftsmanship and affordable solutions, we specialize in home repairs, remodeling, and professional painting services.

Our team is dedicated to turning your house into a home with expertise in flooring, drywall, electrical work, framing, waterproofing, and more. At DeAngelo Construction, your satisfaction is our priority — we take pride in delivering projects that exceed your expectations.
