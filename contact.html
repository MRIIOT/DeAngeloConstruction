What to expect

From call to completion.

Process

  1. Call or write.

    Reach out by phone, text, or email. Tell us what you're thinking — a paragraph is plenty, photos are better, and if you have a deadline, let us know.

    Step one

  2. Site visit.

    We'll schedule a time to come by, look at the space, take measurements, and talk through options. The visit is free, usually takes 20-45 minutes, and doesn't commit you to anything.

    Step two

  3. Written estimate.

    You'll get a line-item quote with materials, labor, and a realistic timeline. If anything doesn't make sense, ask — we'd rather answer ten questions up front than surprise you halfway through.

    Step three

  4. Build.

    Once you're ready, we schedule the work, pull permits where needed, and get started. Clean jobsites, clear communication, and a final walkthrough before we call it done.
