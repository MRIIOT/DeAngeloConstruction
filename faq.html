What services do you offer? +
We provide a full range of home repair and remodeling services including painting, drywall, flooring, electrical, bathroom and basement remodeling, framing, waterproofing, fencing, demolition, and gutter cleaning. See the full list on our Services page.
Do you offer free estimates? +
Yes — we offer free estimates for all our services. Simply contact us and we'll schedule a convenient time to come by, measure the project, and put together an honest quote.
What areas do you serve? +
We proudly serve Barrington, IL and the greater Chicagoland area — from Rockford to Rosemont, Gurnee to Naperville, and most of the towns in between. Full list on the About page.
How long does a typical project take? +
The duration depends on the scope, but we strive to complete every project efficiently without compromising quality. We'll give you a detailed timeline during your consultation — and if it slips, you'll hear from us first, not last.
Are you licensed and insured? +
Yes — DeAngelo Construction is fully licensed and insured, ensuring that your home is in safe and capable hands. Proof of insurance is available on request before any work begins.
