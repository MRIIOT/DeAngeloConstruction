Our services.

At DeAngelo Construction we offer a range of home improvement and remodeling services to meet all your needs. Every project starts with a free estimate — here's what we do.

  1. Exterior & interior painting

    Transform your home with high-quality painting services that breathe new life into both interiors and exteriors — clean prep, cut lines, and finishes that hold up.

    Finishes

  2. Remodeling

    We specialize in transforming bathrooms, basements, kitchens and more into modern, functional spaces — from demo to paint to the last trim nail.

    Build-out

  3. Framing

    Our framing service delivers precise, reliable craftsmanship for a strong foundation on every project — walls that are plumb, square, and ready for the next trade.

    Structure

  4. Flooring

    Expert flooring installation and quality materials for a durable, beautiful finish in any space — LVP, hardwood, tile, laminate.

    Finishes

  5. Drywall & taping

    Professional drywall installation and taping for smooth, flawless walls — hand-sanded to a finish you won't see seams on.

    Finishes

  6. Electrical

    Safe, efficient wiring and reliable installations for any project — new circuits, fixtures, switches, and full rough-ins.

    Systems

  7. Waterproofing

    Protect your property from moisture with long-lasting durability — stop water damage at the foundation before it starts upstairs.

    Envelope

  8. Fencing

    Durable fences to boost your property's security and beauty — multiple styles and materials to suit your needs and the neighborhood.

    Exterior

  9. Demolition

    Safe and efficient demo clears the way for your next project — careful tear-out, dust control, and a tidy site when we're done.

    Site prep

  10. Gutter cleaning

    Clear, debris-free gutters keep water moving off your roof — regular maintenance prevents the kind of damage that gets expensive fast.

    Maintenance
